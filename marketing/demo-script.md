# 60-second demo video (record it with Orbit itself)

Setup: 1080p · 60 fps, Entire Screen, Face Cam on, Mic on. Have a real-looking thing open to demo
(a design file, a dashboard, or Orbit's own repo). Talk naturally; cut the pauses in the editor.
Export, then trim to ~60s in QuickTime (⌘T). Put the MP4 on the landing page and in the X thread.

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Desktop. Press ⌘⇧5. Orbit's bar slides up. | "This is Orbit. Press command-shift-5, like the Mac screenshot bar." |
| 0:05 | Click Face Cam. Circle appears bottom-left. Drag it to the top-right, pinch to shrink. | "Your camera goes in a circle. Drag it anywhere. Scroll to resize." |
| 0:12 | Click Selected Portion, draw a frame around the demo window. Label shows 1920 × 1080. | "Record the whole screen, or just an area. Sixteen-by-nine snaps to exactly ten-eighty-p." |
| 0:20 | Click Record. Do a 10-second mini demo, deliberately letting the circle cover a button. | "Here's the thing every recorder gets wrong…" (point at the covered button) "…my face is on top of what I'm showing." |
| 0:32 | Press ⌘⇧5. Editor opens. | "With Loom, you'd re-record. With Orbit, you stop…" |
| 0:36 | Drag the dashed circle in the preview away from the button. Scrub the timeline. | "…and move it. The screen and the camera are separate files, so nothing is baked in." |
| 0:44 | Toggle Mirror, drag the Size slider, click Follow my recording to show the path replay. | "Resize it, mirror it, hide it, or play back the exact path you dragged." |
| 0:50 | Click Export MP4. Finder reveals the file. | "Export one MP4. Nothing was uploaded; it's in Movies, Orbit, on your Mac." |
| 0:55 | Landing page in the browser. | "Free beta, open source, link below." |

Notes
- Keep the cursor slow; viewers watch the face circle, not the pointer.
- The "covered button" moment is the whole pitch. Make it obvious.
- Thumbnail for X/PH: the editor frame with the dashed circle mid-drag.
