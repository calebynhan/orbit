Leaving Loom? Here's an honest look at Orbit.

Loom's Business plan is $18 per user per month in 2026, prices on the top plan went up by a third in January, and in February Atlassian retired the free “Creator Lite” seat, so everyone in a workspace is billed. If you mostly record quick videos for a few people, that's a lot of money for a recorder.

What Orbit is

A free, open-source Mac app. You press ⌘⇧5, get a bar like the macOS screenshot bar with a Face Cam button, and record with your camera in a draggable circle. When you stop, an editor opens where you can move, resize, mirror, hide, or re-record the face cam, then export an MP4.

What you gain

  • No subscription. The beta is free; the finished app will be a one-time purchase.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac. No account, no upload, no viewer tracking. Good for client work, legal, healthcare, or anything under NDA.
  • Fix the face cam afterwards. Loom bakes the circle into the video. Orbit keeps it separate, so you can move it off the thing it's covering.
  • Exact output. A 16:9 area at 1080p is exactly 1920×1080 at a constant 60 fps.
  • You can read the code. It's on GitHub under AGPL-3.0.

What you lose

  • Share links. Orbit exports a file. You share it the way you share any file. Hosted links may come later as an optional extra.
  • Viewer analytics, comments, and transcripts. None of that exists in Orbit.
  • Windows and mobile. Orbit is Mac-only (macOS 15 or later).
  • Polish. It's a beta from one person. Expect rough edges and report them.

Who it's for

Developers and designers sending demos to teammates, freelancers walking clients through work, teachers recording lessons, and anyone who wants their face in a corner without paying monthly. If your team lives on Loom's share links and comment threads, Orbit isn't a replacement yet.

Switching

  1. Download your existing Loom videos from each video's menu before cancelling (Loom keeps them only while you have an account).
  2. Download Orbit and follow the one-time opening steps.
  3. Let Orbit take over ⌘⇧5 when it asks.
