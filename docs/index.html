Your face, after the fact

Orbit records the screen and the camera as separate files and remembers where the circle was. In the editor you can replay your path, pin the circle somewhere new, resize it, mirror it, or hide it — then export one MP4.

Exact 1080p framing

Record the whole screen or draw a frame. The 16:9 lock snaps to exact steps, so a selected area comes out at precisely 1920×1080 at a constant 60 fps — no black bars, no stretching.

Stays on your Mac

Recordings go to ~/Movies/Orbit. There's no account, no upload, and no viewer analytics. If you work with client or patient data, that's the whole point.

Forgot the camera? Add it later

Recorded without your face? The editor plays the video back while it records your camera and mic, then overlays it. Or import a clip you already have.

Opening the beta

The beta isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet (that's a $99/year fee we'll pay once people want this), so macOS blocks it the first time. One-time fix:

  1. Open the DMG and drag Orbit to Applications, then double-click it. macOS will refuse.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Orbit was blocked”, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Orbit appears in your menu bar (⏺) and asks for Camera, Microphone, and Screen Recording permission.
  4. Say yes when Orbit offers to take over ⌘⇧5 from the macOS screenshot bar. You can give it back from the menu at any time.

Questions

Is it really free?

The beta is free, and the source code always will be. When Orbit leaves beta, the ready-built app will be a one-time purchase. No subscription.

Can I share a link like Loom?

Not yet. Orbit exports an MP4 you can drop into Slack, email, or any drive. Hosted share links may come later as an optional extra.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes, on macOS 15 Sequoia or later.

Open an issue on GitHub. Early feedback decides what gets built.
