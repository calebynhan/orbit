Orbit

Record your screen with your face in a bubble you can drag around, and move it again after you stop. Local, free, and open source.

Your shortcut

Press it anywhere to open the capture bar. Press it again to stop recording.

On Wayland your desktop may not let apps grab keys. If the shortcut doesn’t work, add one in your system’s keyboard settings that runs orbit --toggle.

How it works

  1. Press . The bar opens at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Pick Entire Screen or Selected Portion. Drag your face cam where you want it. Scroll on it to resize, right-click for shape and outline.
  3. Click Record. Stop with the shortcut, the stop button, or the tray icon.
  4. The editor opens. Move or restyle the face cam, re-record it, then export an MP4.
